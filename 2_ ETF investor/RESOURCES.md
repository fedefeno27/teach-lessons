# ETF Investing (UK) — Resources

Curated, high-trust sources for this workspace. Knowledge in lessons is drawn from here,
not from memory. Wisdom comes from the communities in the second section.

---

## Knowledge

### UK-specific foundations

- [Monevator — Passive investing guide](https://monevator.com/new-guide-to-passive-investing/)
  The best UK-specific passive investing writing there is. Independent, numerate, no product
  sales. **Use for:** portfolio construction from a UK wrapper perspective, platform costs,
  anything where US advice would mislead a UK investor.
- [Monevator — Slow and Steady model portfolio](https://monevator.com/passive-investing-model-portfolio/)
  A real, publicly tracked portfolio running since 2011 with quarterly updates. **Use for:**
  seeing rebalancing and contribution routing applied over a full market cycle, including
  drawdowns, rather than in theory.
- [Monevator — 9 lazy portfolios for UK investors](https://monevator.com/9-lazy-portfolios-for-uk-passive-investors-2010/)
  **Use for:** comparing named allocation archetypes and the reasoning behind each weight.
- [Monevator — The ISA allowance: how it works](https://monevator.com/annual-isa-allowance/)
  **Use for:** wrapper rules, allowance mechanics, flexible-ISA behaviour.

### Tax, domicile and wrapper mechanics

- [Bogleheads wiki — Nonresident alien investors and Ireland domiciled ETFs](https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Nonresident_alien_investors_and_Ireland_domiciled_ETFs)
  The canonical explanation of why Irish domicile beats Luxembourg and US domicile for
  non-US investors. **Use for:** withholding tax logic, treaty rates, estate tax exposure.
- [Bogleheads wiki — Nonresident alien's ETF domicile decision table](https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Nonresident_alien%27s_ETF_domicile_decision_table)
  **Use for:** a decision table when domicile is genuinely ambiguous.
- [Bogleheads wiki — Investing from the UK](https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/UK_investing)
  **Use for:** UK wrapper specifics written for an investing-literate audience.
- [Bogleheads wiki — Non-US investor's guide to navigating US tax traps](https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Non-US_investor%27s_guide_to_navigating_US_tax_traps)
  **Use for:** PRIIPs, PFIC, US estate tax — the traps that make US tickers the wrong answer here.
- [HMRC — IFM12410: Offshore funds, reporting funds](https://gov.uk/hmrc-internal-manuals/investment-funds/ifm12410)
  Primary source. **Use for:** what UK Reporting Fund Status legally is. Matters only outside
  ISA/SIPP, but matters enormously there.
- [BlackRock — UK Reporting Fund Status and Excess Reportable Income](https://www.blackrock.com/uk/solutions/adviser-resources/reporting-fund-status)
  **Use for:** how ERI actually shows up in a tax return on unwrapped holdings.
- [State Street — US-domiciled ETFs vs Irish-domiciled UCITS ETFs for non-US investors](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/institutional/insights/considerations-for-non-us-investors-us-etfs-vs-irish-ucits)
  Issuer paper, but the tax arithmetic is sound and sourced. **Use for:** the quantified
  withholding comparison. Read with an awareness of who wrote it.
- [Fidelity UK — 2026/27 tax allowances](https://www.fidelity.co.uk/markets-insights/personal-finance/personal-finance/20262027-tax-allowances-whats-changed/)
  **Use for:** current-year allowance figures. Re-check every April; these date fast.

### Fund quality and cost forensics

- [justETF Academy — Make the right ETF selection](https://www.justetf.com/uk/academy/make-the-right-etf-selection.html)
  **Use for:** the selection checklist — fund size, spread, replication, distribution policy.
- [justETF Academy — ETF liquidity: what you need to know](https://www.justetf.com/en/news/etf/etf-liquidity-what-you-need-to-know.html)
  **Use for:** why AUM and spread matter more than headline volume.
- [justETF Academy — Synthetic replication of ETFs](https://www.justetf.com/en/academy/synthetic-replication-of-etfs.html)
  **Use for:** how swaps work, collateral, the UCITS 10% counterparty cap.
- [ETF.com — Understanding tracking difference and tracking error](https://www.etf.com/sections/etf-basics/understanding-tracking-difference-and-tracking-error)
  **Use for:** the distinction between the two, and why tracking difference is the outcome
  metric while TER is only an input.
- [Morningstar — ETF tracking difference and tracking error](https://www.morningstar.com/business/insights/blog/funds/etf-tracking-difference-error)
  **Use for:** independent framing of the same, plus securities lending's effect.
- [ETF Stream — Ireland vs Luxembourg: domiciling ETFs in Europe](https://www.etfstream.com/education/regulation/ireland-vs-luxembourg-domiciling-etfs-in-europe)
  Trade press, independent of issuers. **Use for:** the structural reason SICAVs miss the treaty rate.

### Portfolio construction and behaviour

- [Vanguard — Rational rebalancing: an analytical approach](https://www.vanguardmexico.com/content/dam/intl/americas/documents/latam/en/2022/10/mx-sa-2558523-rational-rebalancing-an-analytical-approach.pdf)
  Research paper, not marketing. **Use for:** the evidence that rebalancing frequency barely
  matters and tolerance bands beat calendars on cost.
- [Vanguard — Rebalancing your portfolio](https://investor.vanguard.com/investor-resources-education/portfolio-management/rebalancing-your-portfolio)
  **Use for:** the practitioner-level summary of the above.
- [Bogleheads wiki — Rebalancing](https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Rebalancing)
  **Use for:** the 5/25 band rule and worked examples.
- [Vanguard — How to invest a lump sum of money](https://investor.vanguard.com/investor-resources-education/news/lump-sum-investing-versus-cost-averaging-which-is-better)
  **Use for:** lump sum vs cost averaging evidence. Directly relevant — Federico has both a
  lump sum and monthly contributions.
- [MSCI — Foundations of Factor Investing (Bender et al.)](https://www.msci.com/documents/1296102/1336482/Foundations_of_Factor_Investing.pdf)
  Index-provider research, rigorous and widely cited. **Use for:** factor definitions and
  construction before any decision about tilts.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Monevator comment threads](https://monevator.com/)
  The comments under Monevator articles are unusually high-signal for a UK audience — regulars
  include people who have run passive portfolios for decades. **Use for:** sanity-checking a
  UK allocation, platform and wrapper questions.
- [Bogleheads forum — Non-US Investing](https://www.bogleheads.org/forum/viewforum.php?f=22)
  Strongly moderated against speculation. Post a full portfolio and get it critiqued properly.
  **Use for:** a structured second opinion on an Investment Policy Statement. Read the
  "Asking Portfolio Questions" format before posting.
- [The Lemon Fool](https://www.lemonfool.co.uk/)
  UK shares, investment and personal finance forums. Older, experienced UK membership.
  **Use for:** UK platform experience, wrapper edge cases, decumulation.

> Community note: the point of these is to test conclusions against people who have lived
> through more market cycles than either of us. Post the *reasoning*, not just the tickers —
> the reasoning is what gets usefully attacked.
