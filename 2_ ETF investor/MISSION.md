# Mission: ETF Investing (UK)

## Why

Federico already owns ETFs but bought them without a system — no documented weighting rule,
no rebalancing policy, no way to tell whether the funds overlap or leak money. The goal is to
turn an ad-hoc collection of holdings into a **deliberate portfolio with a written policy**
that compounds over a 10–20 year horizon and ends in financial independence, held entirely
inside a UK Stocks &amp; Shares ISA.

The deeper outcome: being able to read any ETF, any allocation proposal, or any piece of
market news and decide — with reasons — whether it changes the portfolio or not. Most of the
time the correct answer is "it doesn't", and knowing *why* is what stops a 15-year plan from
being destroyed in a bad month.

## Success looks like

- Can open any ETF's KID and factsheet and extract, in under five minutes: domicile, replication
  method, accumulating vs distributing, total real cost, and whether it duplicates something
  already held.
- Has a written **Investment Policy Statement**: target weights, the rule that sets those weights,
  rebalancing bands, contribution routing, and the pre-committed response to a 30%+ drawdown.
- Can compute portfolio-level overlap and true look-through exposure, not just count funds.
- Can compute look-through sector and geographic exposure across every fund held, and state
  where the portfolio is concentrated without opening a single factsheet twice.
- Can tell the difference between a deliberate tilt and an accidental one, and can say what
  each tilt is a bet on, in one sentence, without jargon.
- Can read a fund launch, a factor claim, or a market headline and classify it as signal,
  noise, or a sales pitch — then do nothing when doing nothing is correct.
- Deploys the lump sum and directs monthly contributions according to the policy, not to mood.

## Constraints

- **UK resident**, investing through a **Stocks &amp; Shares ISA only** — no SIPP. This is a
  simplification worth exploiting: inside an ISA there is no capital gains tax, no dividend
  tax, no excess reportable income, and UK Reporting Fund Status is irrelevant. **Restructuring
  therefore costs only spreads and dealing fees, not tax.** Very few investors get to
  reorganise a portfolio this cheaply.
- Two sleeves by choice: a main portfolio and a deliberately separate "experiment" sleeve used
  for learning. The separation is a feature; it needs a size cap and a written purpose.
- **Europe overweight is deliberate**, and confirmed as such. The open question is not whether
  to have it but whether the current implementation expresses it.
- Starting point: owns ETFs, no system. Comfortable with the idea of index investing; wants
  the advanced layer, not a beginner's introduction.
- Explicitly asked not to be kept at basic level — lessons should scale into portfolio
  construction maths, factor evidence, and fund-document forensics.
- Wants to know how to read ETF and market news in a way that actually improves the portfolio.

## Out of scope

- Stock picking and individual company analysis.
- Day trading, options, leveraged or inverse ETFs as core holdings.
- Crypto.
- Non-UK tax regimes.
- Regulated personal financial advice — this workspace teaches method and evidence so
  Federico can make his own decisions, and says so on every lesson.
