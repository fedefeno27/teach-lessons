# Head of Design (Industrial Design) — Resources

## Knowledge

### Communicating & defending decisions
- [Book: _Articulating Design Decisions_ (2nd ed.) — Tom Greever, O'Reilly](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/articulating-design-decisions/9781492079194/)
  The single best book on the meeting where your design lives or dies. Premise: how you talk
  about the work matters more than the work. Use for: preparing for reviews, responding to
  feedback in the moment, handling the stakeholder who "just doesn't like it."

### Business & commercial fluency
- [Glossary: Gross Margin — First Round Review](https://review.firstround.com/glossary/gross-margin/)
  Tight, trustworthy definition: `gross margin = (net sales − COGS) ÷ net sales × 100`, what
  sits inside COGS, and why leadership reads margin as operational health. Benchmarks:
  software 70–90%, manufacturing/retail 20–50%. Use for: the arithmetic behind any cost argument.
- [Reference: Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) — AccountingTools](https://www.accountingtools.com/articles/what-is-cogs-cost-of-goods-sold.html)
  What actually counts as COGS: direct materials, direct labour, factory overhead, production
  supplies. Use for: knowing which of your design decisions land in COGS and which don't.

### Design leadership & org building
- [Book: _Org Design for Design Orgs_ — Peter Merholz & Kristin Skinner, O'Reilly](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/org-design-for/9781491938393/)
  How in-house design teams are structured, staffed, and made to matter. Use for: when the
  conversation turns to headcount, team shape, and where design reports.
- [Book: _The Making of a Manager_ — Julie Zhuo](https://www.juliezhuo.com/book/manager.html)
  The canonical first-time-design-manager book, organised around Purpose, People, Process.
  Use for: the first 90 days with reports, 1:1s, feedback, hiring.
- [Book: _Design Leadership_ — Richard Banfield, O'Reilly](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/design-leadership/9781491929193/)
  Interviews with design-firm and in-house leaders on how they actually run things.
  Use for: comparing leadership models before choosing your own.

### Design rationale (concept)
- [Design Rationale — WPI AI Research Group survey](https://web.cs.wpi.edu/Research/aidg/DR-Rpt98.html)
  Academic survey of design rationale as a field: a rationale captures not just the reason for a
  decision but the alternatives considered, the trade-offs weighed, and the argument that
  selected one. Use for: the theory behind why "why is it better than the alternative?" is the
  question that does the work.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [Leading Design (Clearleft)](https://leadingdesign.com/) — Conference + year-round network
  explicitly for people moving from doing design to leading it. UK-headquartered, high signal.
  Use for: the transition itself, and peers one step ahead of you.
- [IDSA — Industrial Designers Society of America](https://www.idsa.org/) — The flagship
  industrial design professional body; runs a Design Leadership Bootcamp aimed at senior
  designers stepping up to management. Use for: ID-specific leadership framing and events.
- [Core77 Discussion Boards](https://www.core77.com/) — Long-running industrial design
  community, partnered with IDSA. Use for: portfolio and career-move critique from working IDs.

## Gaps

- **Hardware-specific design leadership.** Almost all design-leadership writing is digital/UX.
  Nothing found yet that treats leading an *industrial* design team — tooling budgets, supplier
  relationships, physical prototyping cadence — as its own discipline. Keep hunting; in the
  meantime, translate rather than copy.
- **Design portfolio for leadership roles.** No trusted source yet on what a Head of Design
  interview portfolio contains (decisions and org outcomes, not renders).
- **UK-specific.** Salary bands, typical org shapes, and hiring norms for UK manufacturing
  design teams are unmapped.
