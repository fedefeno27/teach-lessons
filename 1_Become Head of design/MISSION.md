# Mission: Becoming Head of Design (Industrial Design)

## Why
Federico is a Senior Industrial Designer with no direct reports who wants to run a design
function — either by growing into it where he is now, or by being the obvious external hire
when a Head of Design role opens. The bottleneck is not craft. It is the set of behaviours
that make a designer legible to the people who hand out that job: talking in money, leading
people who aren't him, setting direction, and telling the story of a decision so it survives
the room.

## Success looks like
- Can state any design decision as a one-sentence business argument before showing a single render.
- Can read and argue from a product P&L line: COGS, gross margin, tooling, unit volume.
- Can run a design critique that improves someone else's work without taking it over.
- Can write and defend a 2–3 year design direction for a product portfolio, including what to stop doing.
- Has a portfolio of *decisions* (not just outcomes) he can narrate on demand in an interview or a board review.
- Is asked into commercial conversations early, rather than briefed after they conclude.

## Constraints
- ~30 minutes per week. Lessons must be self-contained, completable in one sitting, and resumable cold.
- Learning must be practisable on live work — no hypothetical case studies where a real one exists.
- Hardware/physical product context. Digital-product design leadership material must be translated, not copy-pasted.

## Out of scope (for now)
- Founding an agency or consultancy.
- Deep finance beyond what is needed to argue a product decision.
- Formal HR/legal process (redundancy, contracts) until people leadership is actually in hand.
